## 1. Brand Essence & Messaging

Zatiaras Juice is positioned as a daily fruit-drink brand that should feel fresh, close, accessible, and product-led.

### Message hierarchy

- **Core customer proof:** `Buahnya Berasa.`
- **Supporting assortment line:** `Jus Apa Aja Ada.`
- **Product signature:** `Kentalnya Beneran.`
- **Value proof:** `Worth it rasanya.`
- **Trust support:** `Fresh dibuat langsung.`

### Hero products shown

- Jus Alpukat
- Alpukat Kocok
- Jus Mangga

The communication system emphasizes real fruit taste, product density, variety, value, and freshness.

## 2. Logo System & Protection

The identity uses three primary logo configurations:

1. **Standalone Z-Cup**
   - For icons, avatars, favicons, compact spaces, and secondary use.

2. **Primary Horizontal Lockup**
   - Z-Cup symbol + `ZATIARAS JUICE`
   - Main/default logo for most applications.

3. **Wordmark Only**
   - Used when the symbol is not required or when a simplified lockup is more suitable.

### Core rules

- Always maintain clear space.
- Use official assets only.
- Do not stretch, compress, rotate, rearrange, or redraw the logo.
- Do not crowd the logo with text, imagery, or other graphic elements.

## 3. Color System

### Core brand colors

- **Heritage Magenta**
- **Fruit Cream**
- **Fruit Ink**
- **Dark Berry**
- **Deep Green**

### Product accent colors

- Avocado Green
- Mango
- Coral
- Other product-specific colors may be used as accents.

### Color usage modes

#### Mode A — Magenta Brand
Used for:
- campaign
- promo
- launch
- attention-driven communication

#### Mode B — Cream Information
Used for:
- menu
- price list
- operational information
- educational/informational material

#### Mode C — Product / Photo
Used for:
- product-first social media
- delivery thumbnails
- food/product content

### Color principle

- Magenta owns recognition.
- Cream owns information space.
- Product colors support appetite and product differentiation.
- Product accents do not replace the core brand colors.

## 5. Graphic Language System

Three major graphic devices create the brand's visual language.

### Z-Flow
A fluid, expressive shape derived from the Z-Cup symbol.

Functions:
- motion
- energy
- visual ownership
- layout framing

### Cup-Lip Bar
A simple horizontal graphic element inspired by the lip/rim of the cup.

Functions:
- section divider
- highlight
- visual organization

### Z-Crop
A cropped portion of the Z-Cup symbol used as a bold graphic frame.

Functions:
- framing
- depth
- layout variation
- recognizable brand texture

## 6. Product Imagery & Cup Branding

### Photography principles

1. Product first.
2. Show natural fruit texture.
3. Use clear cups.
4. Use one-side logo application.
5. Favor cream-dominant backgrounds.
6. Use real, appetizing product imagery.

The document shows multiple beverage variants to prove the logo remains recognizable against different drink colors.

## 8. Application QA — Do & Don't

### Do

- Use official SVG assets.
- Maintain original logo proportions.
- Use the official horizontal lockup where appropriate.
- Maintain clear space.
- Use approved typography.
- Use realistic product imagery.
- Use approved brand messages.
- Apply correct cup branding.

### Don't

- Redraw or AI-recreate the logo.
- Stretch, skew, rotate, or distort the logo.
- Recolor it arbitrarily.
- Stack or rearrange logo components.
- Place decorative clutter too close to the logo.
- Use unrealistic or fake-looking product photography.
- Make unapproved health or product claims.

## 10. Production & Physical Proof

Brand production is not considered complete until it works physically.

### Required proof types

- Cup proof
- Signage proof
- Print and color proof
- Small-size digital test

The brand should be checked for:

- readability
- contrast
- logo clarity
- print quality
- physical material compatibility
- small-size recognition

Mass production should not proceed before physical proof is approved.

---

## 11. Asset Handoff, Governance & Final QA

The guideline closes with rules for:

- official asset handoff
- file naming
- vendor usage
- rejection criteria
- final QA

### Core governance principle

All touchpoints should use official assets. The logo must not be recreated, redrawn, modified, or improvised.
