# Zatiaras Juice — Application & Rollout Kit

**Source:** `Zatiaras_Juice_Application_and_Rollout_Kit_V1.0_12_Halaman.pdf`  
**Function:** Operational application guide for digital channels, outlet touchpoints, packaging, campaigns, and branch rollout.

---

## 1. Purpose

The Application & Rollout Kit explains how the Zatiaras Juice brand should move from a visual identity into real customer-facing execution.

Its scope covers:

- digital channels
- marketplace platforms
- Google Business Profile
- outlet signage
- packaging
- campaign templates
- branch rollout

The central idea is:

**one brand, many touchpoints, consistent customer experience.**

---

## 2. Touchpoint Architecture

The customer journey is presented as a continuous cycle.

### Explore
Customers first encounter the brand through general discovery and menu browsing.

### Discover
Channels include:
- Instagram Feed
- Instagram Story
- Instagram Reels

### Order
Ordering happens through:
- GoFood
- GrabFood
- other supported ordering channels

### Choose
Google Business Profile helps customers:
- find the outlet
- inspect information
- compare
- navigate

### Enjoy
In-store experience includes:
- menu
- ordering
- service
- product consumption

### Packaging & Takeaway
The brand continues through:
- cup
- takeaway packaging
- carry bags

### Outlet Signage
Physical brand recognition around the outlet.

### Campaign & Promo
Used to:
- promote products
- announce offers
- attract customer attention

### Repeat & Review
Satisfied customers are encouraged to:
- return
- review
- recommend

### Experience
The entire cycle should reinforce a consistent Zatiaras brand experience.

## 3. Instagram Feed System

The feed system is designed to create a consistent visual grid.

### Content patterns shown

- brand statement
- hero product
- assortment
- product photography
- freshness message
- campaign message

### Feed guidance

- format: 1080 × 1080 px
- use official templates
- prioritize one hero product
- use one main message
- use light/simple CTA
- keep branding consistent

The objective is to make products look appetizing without sacrificing brand recognition.

## 6. GoFood / GrabFood Product System

The goal is a marketplace listing that is clear, appetizing, and operationally accurate.

### Product listing checklist

- use real product photos
- use clear product names
- use short descriptions
- use actual price
- follow the outlet/platform price
- use current platform rating
- use current delivery information
- avoid invented claims or inaccurate information

This system aims to improve customer confidence and conversion.

---

## 7. Google Business Profile System

Google Business Profile is treated as a major local discovery channel.

### Best practices

1. Keep operational hours accurate.
2. Use real outlet photos.
3. Actively respond to customer reviews.
4. Complete all profile information.
5. Use a branded cover photo.

Information should remain accurate and up to date.

## 8. Menu / Price Communication

The menu system should be:

- simple
- clear
- easy to scan
- visually appetizing
- consistent with the brand

The example groups products into clear menu categories such as:

- Jus Buah
- Favorit

Each item should have:
- clear name
- clear price
- consistent typographic hierarchy

The menu is a communication tool, not just a price list.

## 9. Outlet Signage System

Five signage types are shown.

### Main Storefront Sign
Primary identity visible from the storefront.

### Neon Sign
Used to create recognition, especially at night.

### Hanging Sign
Useful for side visibility from passing traffic.

### Counter Sign
Brand reinforcement at the point of transaction.

### Window Sticker
Additional visibility on glass surfaces.

### Principle

Signage should remain:
- recognizable
- readable
- consistent
- proportional to viewing distance

## 10. Packaging / Takeaway & QR Review

The brand extends beyond the drink cup.

Shown applications include:

- cup
- paper bag
- food wrapping
- takeaway materials
- Google Review QR card

### Checklist

1. Use official packaging.

### Core Message

- strongest proposition
- main campaign message

### Support Message
Supports the main message with:
- variety
- product benefit
- additional context

### Trust Cue
Examples:
- freshness
- real ingredients
- made directly

### Value Cue
Communicates:
- value

---

## 13. Campaign Do & Don't

### Do

- focus on one campaign idea
- keep product photography strong
- use clear CTA
- maintain visual consistency
- use approved brand language

### Don't

- overload the design
- use too many competing messages
- use unsupported claims
- distort the logo
- use unrelated visual styles
- compromise product realism

## 14. Branch Rollout Checklist

Rollout is divided into five stages.

### Stage 1 — Pre-Launch

Prepare:
- brand asset pack
- cup proof
- signage proof
- vendor files
- print/packaging requirements

### Stage 2 — Digital Setup

Prepare:
- Instagram profile
- highlights
- Google Business Profile
- GoFood / GrabFood presence
- WhatsApp/catalog information

### Stage 3 — Outlet Setup

Prepare:
- signage
- counter materials
- menu
- QR review
- packaging
- cleaning / hygiene readiness
- customer-facing materials

### Stage 4 — Launch Week

Execute:
- launch content
- social media activation
- hero product communication
- team briefing
- soft launch / opening execution

### Stage 5 — Review Gate

Review:
- logo accuracy
- color consistency
- price consistency
- product-photo consistency
- customer feedback
- outlet review
- Google/Instagram/GoFood presence

If all key elements are in place, the branch can move to:

**Ready for Controlled Rollout**
