# SEO_AI_DISCOVERY.md — Search & AI Discoverability


**Human authority: BRD/PRD/Technical Design under `docs/human/`**
**Baseline:** Reconciled v1.1
## Goal

Make Zatiaras easy for search engines and AI retrieval systems to:
- find,
- understand,
- verify,
- cite/recommend when relevant.

This does not guarantee a "best juice" recommendation.

---

## Entity clarity

Public HTML should clearly establish:

```text
Zatiaras Juice
→ juice / fruit drink business
→ Samarinda
→ Juanda
→ Slamet Riyadi
→ products
```

Do not rely only on slogan/artwork to communicate factual identity.

---

## Public page architecture

Important indexable pages:
- homepage,
- menu,
- hero product pages,
- Samarinda landing page,
- Juanda page,
- Slamet Riyadi page,
- locations,
- concise about/contact.

Private/transaction pages should not be search landing pages.

---

## Rendering

Critical facts must be available in initial/server-readable HTML.

Do not hide important information only inside:
- client-only rendering,
- canvas,
- images,
- animation.

---

## Structured data

Use only when truthful and supported:
- `Organization`
- `LocalBusiness`
- `Product`
- `Offer`
- `BreadcrumbList`

Structured data must match visible page content.

---

## Technical baseline

- page titles
- useful descriptions
- canonical URLs
- sitemap.xml
- robots policy
- internal links
- correct status codes
- mobile performance
- semantic HTML

---

## Brand dependency

Structured data and factual copy are product/search concerns.

For visual presentation of logo, colors, type, product image, and brand devices:
