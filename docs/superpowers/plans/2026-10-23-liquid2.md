# Liquid 2 Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A scroll-driven, real-time 3D simulated magenta wave that collides with and wraps a product image front and back, then rises to cover the screen, previewed at `/qa/liquid2`.

**Architecture:** A shallow-water height field over the floor plane (x by depth z) plus a spray particle system are stepped on the CPU in typed arrays with fixed, seeded steps (Liquid 1's pattern). The drink is a solid cylinder obstacle in the grid. A WebGL2 renderer draws the surface mesh, merges spray via screen-space fluid rendering, and draws the drink image as a depth-tested quad at the cylinder's depth so the liquid occludes it per pixel.

**Tech Stack:** SvelteKit, Svelte 5 runes, TypeScript, WebGL2, Vitest, playwright-core. Run commands through `rtk`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-liquid2-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Do not modify any Liquid 1 file: `src/lib/liquid1/**`, `src/lib/components/Liquid1Splash.svelte`, `src/routes/qa/liquid1/**`. Importing from `$lib/liquid1/wave` (e.g. `liquidBackingSize`) is allowed.
- Do not touch the homepage (`src/routes/+page.svelte`) or `$lib/liquid/**`.
- No new runtime or dev dependency.
- Drawing buffer cap: 900,000 px desktop, 450,000 px touch (`liquidBackingSize`). 60 FPS cap. Sustained overload switches to the still fallback.
- Stop drawing when offscreen, when the document is hidden, and after the covered endpoint settles; restart on resize and reverse scroll; handle `webglcontextlost`.
- Still mode on: WebGL2 unavailable, reduced motion, data saver, overload.
- Backward scroll drains: the sim always advances forward in time; level and drive follow `progress` down. No snapshots.
- All randomness from a seeded PRNG; fixed sim step `1/120` s with at most 8 substeps per frame (excess time dropped).
- Scene units: drink height = 1.0. Calm surface at y = 0. Drink base at y = -0.15.
- Camera: fixed, raised and tilted down 18°. Cover = surface above camera height + 0.25.
- Canvas `aria-hidden="true"`; drink `<img>` keeps `alt`; page readable without JavaScript.
- Route `/qa/liquid2` is `noindex,nofollow`.

## Review Focus

1. Fast flick from top to bottom and back, or a tab hidden for minutes then shown: huge `dt` and energy spikes must not produce NaN, exploding heights, or a particle flood. Expected: bounded heights, particle count ≤ cap. Test in Task 2 and Task 4.
2. Resize or orientation change mid-sequence: drink re-placed and obstacle updated without resetting the liquid to calm or leaving liquid inside the cylinder. Test in Task 4.
3. Drink image slow or failing to load: liquid still renders live; no WebGL error; DOM `<img>` alt still present. Test in Task 6.
4. `EXT_color_buffer_float` missing: screen-space passes fall back to RGBA8-encoded depth; still `live`. Test in Task 6.
5. Particle budget exhausted during the wrap: new emissions dropped, not overwriting live drops mid-flight in a visible pop. Test in Task 3.

### Task 2: Shallow-water height field with cylinder obstacle

**Files:**
- Create: `src/lib/liquid2/shallow-water.ts`
- Test: `src/lib/liquid2/shallow-water.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `Tier`, `Drink` from Task 1.
- Produces:
  - `const SIM_STEP = 1 / 120`, `const MAX_SUBSTEPS = 8`
  - `interface Domain { x0: number; x1: number; z0: number; z1: number }` — world extent of the grid (front edge `z1` just inside the near clip, back edge `z0` well behind the drink).
  - `interface Breaking { x: number; z: number; y: number; vx: number; vy: number; vz: number; strength: number }`
  - `class ShallowWater`
    - `constructor(tier: Tier, domain: Domain, seed?: number)`
    - `readonly cols: number; readonly rows: number`
    - `height: Float32Array` (surface offset from mean level, `cols*rows`), `velX`, `velZ`: `Float32Array`
    - `solid: Uint8Array` — 1 inside the obstacle
    - `version: number` — increments on every substep
    - `setObstacle(drink: Drink | null): void` — rebuilds `solid`; cells that become solid get height 0 and zero velocity, and their displaced height is spread to the nearest fluid ring so volume is preserved.
    - `advance(dt: number, drive: number): Breaking[]` — fixed substeps; `drive` 0..1 (clamped, NaN → 0) scales swell injection from the left and right edges (opposite phase) and wind; returns breaking events from this call.
    - `impulse(x: number, z: number, radius: number, strength: number): void`
    - `deposit(x: number, z: number, volume: number, vy: number): void` — particle landing.
    - `sample(x: number, z: number): number` — bilinear height, 0 outside.
- Scheme (builder's choice within this): linearised shallow water on a collocated grid with semi-implicit velocity update, wave speed `c = 1.6` world units/s, per-cell damping ramp toward x and z edges (absorbing, like `SurfaceWaves`), reflective zero-normal-velocity on `solid` cells. Breaking when local steepness `|∇h| > 0.55` or upward acceleration exceeds `9.0` u/s²; per-cell cooldown 0.35 s so one crest emits a burst, not a stream. Run-up against solid cells counts toward steepness.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests:**
  - `wind only stays calm`: 10 s at `drive 0` → max |height| in (0.002, 0.08), zero breaking events.
  - `drive builds colliding swells that break`: 10 s at `drive 1` → max |height| > 0.35 and ≥ 1 breaking event.
  - `obstacle blocks flow`: impulse placed 0.6 units left of a `Drink` at the centre; after 1.5 s the height at the mirrored point directly behind the cylinder (on the line through the impulse and drink centre) is smaller than at an equal-distance point not shadowed; `solid` cells keep height 0.
  - `volume is conserved by setObstacle`: sum(height) before and after `setObstacle` differs by < 1e-3 relative.
  - `huge dt is bounded` (Review Focus 1): `advance(30, 1)` then `advance(1/60, 1)` × 60 → every height finite and |h| < 3.
  - `deterministic`: two instances, same seed and inputs, identical `height` arrays after 600 frames.
- [ ] **Step 2:** Run `rtk pnpm vitest run src/lib/liquid2/shallow-water.test.ts` — expect FAIL.
- [ ] **Step 3:** Implement `ShallowWater`.

### Task 3: Spray particles

**Files:**
- Create: `src/lib/liquid2/spray.ts`
- Test: `src/lib/liquid2/spray.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `Breaking`, `ShallowWater`, `Drink`, `SIM_STEP`.
- Produces:
  - `const PARTICLE_STRIDE = 8` — `[x, y, z, vx, vy, vz, radius, age]`
  - `class Spray`
    - `constructor(maxParticles: number, seed?: number)`
    - `data: Float32Array` (`maxParticles * PARTICLE_STRIDE`), `count: number`, `version: number`
    - `emit(events: Breaking[]): void` — per event, a chained sheet: 1 large lead drop (radius 0.045–0.07) plus 3–9 trailing drops (radius shrinking by ~0.8 each) along the velocity, plus fine mist (radius 0.008–0.015) scaled by `strength`. Drop new particles when full; never overwrite live ones (Review Focus 5).
    - `advance(dt: number, field: ShallowWater, drink: Drink | null): void` — gravity 9.8, quadratic air drag by size (smaller drops slow faster), reflect off cylinder with restitution 0.15 and tangential friction 0.6; a particle whose y ≤ `field.sample(x,z)` while descending is removed and `field.deposit(x, z, (4/3)πr³, vy)`. Swap-remove keeps `data` dense.
- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests:**
  - `falls and is absorbed`: one event at y 0.8 above a flat field → `count` reaches 0 within 2 s and field height near landing point changes.
  - `never enters the drink`: 200 particles aimed at the cylinder → every particle's horizontal distance to the drink axis ≥ `radius_drink` − 1e-4 at every step while `y < baseY + height`.
  - `respects the cap` (Review Focus 5): emitting 10× capacity → `count === maxParticles`, and the first emitted particle's position is still advancing (not reset).
  - `small drops decelerate faster`: equal initial speed, after 0.3 s mist speed < lead-drop speed.

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Tasks 1–3.
- Produces:

    - `constructor(tier: Tier, seed?: number)`

    - `disturb(x: number, z: number, strength: number): void` — pointer/touch impulse.
    - `get covered(): boolean` — `level >= coverLevel(cam)` and spray `count === 0`.

### Task 5: WebGL2 renderer

**Files:**
- Create: `src/lib/liquid2/shaders.ts` (GLSL sources as exported strings)
- Create: `src/lib/liquid2/renderer.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `Camera`, `Drink`, `LiquidSim`, `PARTICLE_STRIDE`; `liquidBackingSize` from `$lib/liquid1/wave`.
- Produces:
  - `class Liquid2Renderer`
    - `static create(canvas: HTMLCanvasElement, tier: Tier): Liquid2Renderer | null` — null if WebGL2 missing or any shader fails to compile/link (log the info log via `console.warn`).
    - `resize(width: number, height: number): void`
    - `setDrinkImage(image: HTMLImageElement | ImageBitmap | null): void` — null draws no drink quad.
    - `render(cam: Camera, drink: Drink | null, sim: LiquidSim, time: number): void`
    - `dispose(): void`
- Passes, in order:
  1. Upload `field.height` as `R32F` (or `R16F`) texture when `field.version` changed; upload spray `data` to an instanced buffer when `spray.version` changed.
  2. Opaque drink quad: textured, premultiplied alpha, discard where alpha < 0.5, written to depth at the cylinder's front-face depth (per-pixel, from the cylinder profile) so the liquid in front occludes it and liquid behind is hidden by it.
  3. Surface mesh (`cols × rows` grid, vertex-displaced by height + `level`) to an offscreen depth/normal target.
  4. Spray sphere impostors to a thickness+depth target; bilateral smoothing (2 separable passes, radius scaled by depth); normal reconstruction.
  5. Composite: min-depth merge of surface and smoothed spray, then Liquid 1's material model — opaque magenta, linear-space Fresnel, studio highlights, internal-scattering glow, trough occlusion — copied into `shaders.ts` (copy, do not import, from `$lib/liquid1/wave`).
  - Float render target needs `EXT_color_buffer_float`; when absent, pack depth into RGBA8 (Review Focus 4).
